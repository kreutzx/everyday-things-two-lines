Entry for [Everyday Things, Two Lines, Longer Run](https://make.fun/bounty/u-suga-everyday-things-two-lines-longer-run-f8734479) on make.fun.

`main.py` writes a two-line explanation to `/output/entry.txt`. Line 1 explains
a traffic jam to a 10-year-old in one vivid sentence, built on a single analogy:
Maya pouring 100 marbles through one drinking straw. Line 2 says why the
analogy works — what it maps (cars squeezing toward one narrow road, like
marbles toward the straw's opening) and what it leaves out (drivers can pick
another road, and a jam clears from the front, not the back).

Run: `python3 main.py` — writes `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, within the 400-character gate).
